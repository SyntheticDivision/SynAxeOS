SynAxe OS v-0.0.1-beta for the Bitaxe Gamma 601a 12V only.
Base is official ESP-Miner v2.14.2, not StarMiner.

Install from C:\Projects\synaxe-setup\SynAxeOS with fresh.ps1, then build.ps1 and flash.ps1.
Do not use the folder named "SynAxe OS".

The page on the miner is synaxe-www/index.html.
Tune is 400 to 650 MHz and 1100 to 1225 mV, both in steps of 25.
There is no free-form overclock and no dual-pool mode.
The setup Wi-Fi screen says SynAxe. The network name is synaxe_ plus the last of the MAC.
Stuck test banners are removed. A saved note clears itself.
